Tracks every project in Dublin City Council’s Active Travel Network through quarterly reports to councillors. See which projects are progressing, what stage each is at, and read exactly what each report said in any quarter. Includes a map of projects coloured by stage, with interim projects shown as dotted lines. Built from Active Travel Programme Office and Traffic Department quarterly reports.
