Before You Eat brings together food safety enforcement orders published by the Food Safety Authority of Ireland (closure, prohibition and improvement orders, and prosecutions) going back to 2010. Search by business, town or county, or explore the map. Every record links to the official order document and shows a dated status, and lifted orders are clearly marked as historical. It is independent, not a hygiene rating, and has a corrections and right-of-reply process.
